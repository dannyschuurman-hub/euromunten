Help with Euromunten

Find help with purchases, photo scans and coin values here.

Contact

Email dannyschuurman@gmail.com. We usually reply within a few working days.

Restore your purchase

On your new iPhone, open the offer and tap "Restore purchase". Use the same Apple Account.

Refunds

Full access is a one-off purchase of €14.99, with no subscription. Request a refund from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com; the developer cannot issue refunds.

Photo scans

You get 3 free photo scans and can scan up to 30 photos a day after purchase, with an internet connection. Searching manually by country, coin and year always works and is free.

Wrong value or missing coin?

Email the country, coin and year, and where you saw the different value. Values are estimates from public sources with an as-of date, not a guarantee.

Privacy

Turn off anonymous measurement in the app under "About the data" > "Privacy".
